# 🗺️ Section 4: Maps and Scatterplots

Geographic and relationship visuals on **Amazing Mart EU**: 4,117 orders and 8,047 order lines in 15 European countries.

The report builds a map of sales by country and a scatter plot of sales against profit per customer, then combines them with slicers.
